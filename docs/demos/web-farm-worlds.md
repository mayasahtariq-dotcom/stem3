# Demo: farm worlds, Farm Store and placement

Run the web app with the fake backend, choose a language, then Student.

1. The farm opens in Starter Meadow. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
2. Tap **Fields**: five meadow levels with lock state, XP needed and a progress bar. Only Starter Meadow is open for Asha (120 XP).
3. Tap **Farm Store**: switch categories, pick an item to see it turn in 3D, buy it with gems. Items you can't afford say how many gems you still need.
4. Tap **My items**, then **Place**: the item appears with a green ring. Drag it or tap the ground to move it; the ring turns red with a "Not quite" reason on water, paths, buildings or steep ground. Use Rotate, Cancel or Place here.
5. Tap a placed item to Move it or put it back in My items. Each field saves its own layout.
6. To try another field, give Asha more XP (finish lessons) and the unlock card appears.
